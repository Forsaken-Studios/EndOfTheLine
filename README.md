# EndOfTheLine
 
![image](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2d0aba70-2143-4150-a792-59f1708b1204)

End of the Line is a top-down view survival roguelike with procedurally generated maps that puts you in the shoes of a bearer of an ancient relic that has mysteriously chosen you to wield its power. 

Use it to go deep into the stations and tunnels of a broken world and loot as much as you can to upgrade both yourself and the train that it's now your home. 

But beware... you are not alone down there...

This project was made in Unity 2D as an End Of Master's Degree of Game Development for University of Málaga.

Main features
- Procedural map generation: every expedition is different than the previous one.
- Secure your resources: during your expeditions, collect different resources and stay safe in order to extract and store them in your base for multiple uses.
- Pixel-art artstyle: enjoy the ingame visuals and different animations with a bird's-eye view pixel-art.
